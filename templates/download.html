⚙️ How to Install

Android blocks unknown APKs by default. Follow these steps after downloading:

  • 1
    Tap the downloaded DepEd-LAKAD.apk file in your notifications or Downloads folder.
  • 2
    If prompted, go to Settings → Install unknown apps and allow your browser to install.
  • 3
    Tap Install on the confirmation screen. The app installs in seconds.
  • 4
    Open the app, log in with your existing account credentials, and you're ready.
